The handCut · strung · lit

A chandelier is
a thousand decisions
made by hand.

Cut

Every facet is cut,
then polished

The edge has to hold light on one side and shadow on the other. Cut too shallow and the crystal turns to glass; cut too deep and it swallows the room.

Strung

Each drop is drilled,
checked, then hung

In the order the drawing calls for. One row out of line and the whole run comes back down — there is no machine that makes that judgement.

Lit

It is only finished
once it is lit

Frame, balance, switch on. In the light the piece either holds together or it does not — and that is the moment we judge the work, not the drawing.
